    Level B2

By the end of the sequence

  • I can welcome a customer and start a conversation.
  • I can describe a product and give advice.
  • I can handle payment, an exchange or a refund.
  • I can deal with a complaint politely.
  • I can act out a whole sale without reading my notes.
