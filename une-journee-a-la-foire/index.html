A Day at the Fair: Selling at a UK Country Show

Bac pro vente, anglais

The fair

Every July, thousands of visitors go to the Great Yorkshire Show. Farmers show animals, but small producers also sell cheese, jam, honey and cakes from their stalls. Today you work on a stall: you welcome customers, give prices and make a sale.

Look at the photos. What can you see? Where are the people? What are they doing?

Questions

Touchez une question pour voir la réponse.

Where is the fair?

In Yorkshire, in the north of England.

Who sells at the stalls?

Small producers: cheese, jam, honey, cakes.

What does the seller do first?

He or she greets the customer.

Why do customers like local produce?

It is fresh and made nearby.

Granny's questions

Des questions simples, comme une grand-mère les poserait.

Is it expensive?

C'est cher ? Réponse type : No, it is £4 a jar.

Can I try it?

Je peux goûter ? Réponse : Of course, here is a sample.

Who made it?

Qui l'a fait ? Réponse : We made it on our farm.

Is it fresh?

C'est frais ? Réponse : Yes, it was made this morning.

Is there a toilet near here?

Y a-t-il des toilettes ? Réponse : Yes, next to the café.

Measurements

Conversions utiles : 1 acre = 0,4 ha | 1 mile = 1,6 km | 1 pound (lb) = 454 g | 1 stone = 6,35 kg | 1 pint = 0,57 l | 1 foot = 30 cm

A customer wants a pound of cheese. How many grams is it?

About 454 g.

A jar of honey weighs 340 g. Is it more or less than a pound?

Less than a pound (454 g).

A bottle of apple juice is 1 pint. How many litres?

About 0.57 litres.

The car park is 2 miles away. How many kilometres?

About 3.2 km.

Cheese costs £6 a pound. How much for 2 pounds?

£12.
